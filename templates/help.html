Help & Guide

A simple guide to capturing, organizing and connecting your thoughts.

Get started

Quick Idea

Save a thought as soon as it comes to you. Choosing a project is optional, and you can organize it later.

Capture an idea

Projects

Create projects for any topic. Rename or delete a project whenever needed. Deleting a project keeps its ideas in Unorganised Ideas.

Manage projects

Unorganised Ideas

Ideas without a project live here. Edit an idea to assign it to an existing project or remove its project assignment.

Organize ideas
02

Organise & explore

Global Search

Use the Search page to find ideas by title, text or tag, and find projects by name. Sort results newest or oldest.

Search workspace

Categories & Tags

Categories help classify ideas, while tags give you flexible keywords. Manage both in your Profile page.

Manage categories and tags

Connections & Graph

Inside a project, create a connection between two different ideas and write an optional note about how they relate. The Graph tab shows all ideas.

Open projects

Your workspace

Reminder Notes

Add personal reminders on your dashboard. Notes are saved to your account and can be edited or deleted at any time.

Open dashboard

Account & Privacy

Use the account control at the bottom of the sidebar to edit your details, manage your photo, change your password or log out. Your workspace data belongs to your account.
